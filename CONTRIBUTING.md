# Contributing

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue explaining what you were trying to do, what happened and what you expected.

For payment issues, include the plugin, JetFormBuilder and PHP versions, the relevant form settings, and whether the payment returned through the browser, a webhook or reconciliation. Use anonymised examples and keep credentials and customer details private.

Keep pull requests focused on one change. Describe the problem, how the change solves it and the scenarios you checked. Razorpay Test Mode is useful for reproducing failures and testing changes without charging a customer.

Report sensitive payment or access-control issues privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
